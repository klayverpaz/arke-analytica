Challenges we solve

When software limits the business, it is time to build differently.

  • Off-the-shelf tools that don't fit the rules of your operation.
  • Processes bent to work around system limitations.
  • Low adoption and too much complexity for your teams.
  • Spreadsheets, manual tasks and fragmented data.
  • Recurring licenses that don't match what you need.
  • The need to build technology without hiring an in-house team.

The answer isn't necessarily one more tool. It is the right solution for the right process.

Questions

Questions every business owner asks.

Do I need to understand technology to hire you?

No. You explain your operation your own way, in everyday words. Turning that into a system is our job.

How much does it cost?

The first call has no cost. The diagnostic costs BRL 2,000, at a fixed price. The project has scope, milestones and investment agreed in writing before it starts, based on what the diagnostic found.

Will I own the system?

Usage rights, source code and intellectual property are defined by contract. There is no Arke licensing fee to use the delivered software, when so contracted.

I already use other systems. Will I have to replace everything?

Not necessarily. Often the best solution is to connect the tools your operation already uses and show everything on a single dashboard.

And after delivery, who takes care of it?

Rollout is assisted: we support the go-live and guide your team. After that, maintenance and new features can be contracted on demand.
