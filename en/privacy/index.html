What this site collects

This site uses no cookies, analytics or trackers, and it does not store what you type into the contact form.

Contact form

When you submit the form, your browser opens WhatsApp with a message containing what you filled in: name, company, email, WhatsApp number, a description of the challenge, timeline and, if provided, the investment range. Nothing is sent until you confirm the message in WhatsApp.

We use this information only to reply to you and assess the project. It is not sold or shared for advertising.

Third-party services

Typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts and the page is hosted on GitHub Pages. These services may log technical access data, such as IP address, under their own policies. WhatsApp conversations are subject to WhatsApp's terms.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of or deletion of the data you sent us, under Brazil's General Data Protection Law (Law No. 13,709/2018), by writing to arkeanalytica@gmail.com.
